ABOUT THE DATA

Dates with evidence.

Operating update includes company-scheduled monthly revenue publications, distinct from quarterly earnings; dates are shown without an assumed release time.

Confirmed means a date was verified in an official company announcement, organizer page or participant schedule. A call time is labeled as a call time; it is not an assumed release time.

Estimated date means a published day-level estimate from a linked public calendar such as MarketBeat, Stock Analysis or FT Markets. The source has been checked, but the company has not confirmed the date here. These estimates can change; they are not our own reporting-month guesses. Provider timing is not treated as confirmed.

Reported date means a date from attributable news reporting when primary confirmation has not been verified. Bloomberg reporting read through a public article is attributed to that article; this is not a live Bloomberg feed.

Past scheduled dates remain in their historical calendar month and are labeled Past date. Multi-day events use the full published range. Month-, week- and year-level expectations appear as labeled timing windows, without an invented exact day. Only items with no sourced timing appear in the undated watchlist. List view contains the full history and future timeline, opening near today. Hong Kong is the default display timezone. Choose another city or Browser timezone to convert published event times and their dates. Date-only events have no verified start time, so their published dates are retained. Outlook exports timed events in UTC so Outlook can display them in its own timezone. Date-only events export as all-day entries; uncertain windows export as tentative, transparent all-day ranges. Unknown end times are not replaced with invented durations.

Source discrepancies appear in event briefs. An officially announced date takes precedence over an older provider estimate. Checks older than 14 days are labeled for rechecking. Source checks do not guarantee a date will remain unchanged.

This is a public, read-only calendar maintained through Codex. There is no licensed Bloomberg/Refinitiv API connection or automated source refresh. Public articles and calendars are cited individually. Dividends and routine corporate actions are excluded by default.
