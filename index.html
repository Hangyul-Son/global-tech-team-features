ABOUT THE DATA

Confidence before precision.

Confirmed means an explicit date is supported by an official announcement reviewed by the maintainer. Each entry includes its source and verification time. Confirmation can become stale; check the source before relying on a date.

Estimated month is an editorial planning assumption based on typical quarterly reporting cadence. It is not vendor consensus and has not been checked against a current announcement. No exact day or time is implied; reporting may shift into an adjacent month.

Date unannounced means a monitoring target has no verified date. A potential IPO is a watch item, not a claim that an IPO is scheduled.

The dataset combines manually verified official announcements with unverified planning windows. Source access is partial. The maintainer refresh command reads public official pages and stages possible announcements for review in Codex. No licensed Bloomberg or Refinitiv feed or automated source refresh has been activated.

This is a public, read-only calendar. Event additions, corrections and removals are maintained in the repository through Codex. Dividends and routine corporate actions are excluded by default.
